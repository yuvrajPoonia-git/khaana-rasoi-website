How do I get the website discount?

Tap "Book delivery" or "Book walk-in" on any plan. It opens WhatsApp with a ready message that names your plan, how you want it and the website price. Send it and we take it from there.

Is the food vegetarian?

Yes. Everything on the weekly menu is vegetarian, cooked the way it is at home: parathas, poha, dal, rajma, chhole, seasonal sabzi and chawal.

Can I order just one meal?

Website bookings are for monthly plans only. You can pick a single slot for the month though: breakfast only, lunch only or dinner only.

Do you deliver to my sector?

We deliver only within Sector 104, Noida. If you stay in another sector, you are welcome to take a walk-in plan and eat at the restaurant in Hajipur.
